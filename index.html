Why it plays great

Real rolling physics

The ball truly rolls. Concrete, metal, ice, sand and rubber each have their own grip, bounce and rolling resistance.

50 tracks, 6 worlds

Banked curves, jumps, tunnels, rotating platforms, sliding blocks and sweeper arms. A track takes 30 to 110 seconds.

Easy to learn

One-sentence rules, instant restarts, gold, silver and bronze times on every track, and tracks that unlock in order.

Made for Chromebooks

Automatic quality settings. It holds 60 fps in our tests even on a slowed-down CPU, and the first world loads about 14 MB.

Phones and tablets

On-screen touch controls, plus keyboard and gamepad support.

Family friendly

No violence, chat, ads, external links or name entry. CC0 assets from Poly Haven, original sound and music.
